id: immune
title: Latent virus reactivation in astronauts (Mehta 2017; Rooney 2019)
url: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5445581
---
Spaceflight stress hormones and a weakened immune response let dormant herpes viruses reactivate. More than half of astronauts studied shed at least one herpes virus during flight, and shedding rose with mission length.

Most reactivation causes no symptoms, but some astronauts have had rashes, skin inflammation or cold sores.

Useful onboard signals are body temperature, new skin or lip lesions, and symptom logs. A fever with a new rash should be escalated. Photos help the medical officer and flight surgeon follow changes over time.
