This demo formulary lists medicines the agents may mention. Doses and interactions must come from the real mission formulary and the crew medical officer; TRIX never invents a dose.

Vitamin D3: daily supplement per nutrition requirement. Ibuprofen: pain and headache, as directed by the medical officer. Loratadine: allergy symptoms. Melatonin: sleep aid as directed. Promethazine: motion sickness as directed. Amoxicillin: antibiotic, only when prescribed by the flight surgeon or medical officer.

Any question about combining medicines, doses outside the label, or medicines not on this list is referred to the crew medical officer.
