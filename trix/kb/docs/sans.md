---
id: sans
title: NASA Evidence Report - Spaceflight Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome (SANS)
url: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20180000936
---
SANS is a group of eye and brain changes seen after long-duration spaceflight, thought to be linked to fluid shifting toward the head. Signs include optic disc swelling, folds in the back of the eye, flattening of the eyeball and a shift toward farsightedness.

Most long-duration astronauts show at least one sign; NASA has described it as a leading spaceflight health risk.

Changes can appear without symptoms, so regular checks matter. A simple onboard self-check is an Amsler grid and near-vision card; any new distortion, blur or missing area should be reported to the crew medical officer, and sudden vision loss to the flight surgeon urgently.
