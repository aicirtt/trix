Level 0 normal: keep routine. Level 1 self-care: TRIX suggests a fix and re-checks. Level 2 crew medical officer: review within the day, possibly an exam or procedure. Level 3 flight surgeon: added to the Earth report with priority; live consult only if the delay allows. Level 4 emergency: TRIX agents start immediately and guide a crewmate; never wait for Earth.

A red absolute limit always wins. Two amber signals on the same body part raise the level by one. Persistent amber raises the level by one. Low confidence caps the level at self-care and asks for a re-measurement.

At Mars the one-way delay is about 20 minutes, so flight-surgeon items are queued for the next link window; at the ISS or Moon they can be discussed live.
