NASA-STD-3001 limits an astronaut's total career effective radiation dose from spaceflight to less than 600 mSv, the same for all ages and sexes.

Exposure to solar particle events is limited to 250 mSv effective dose per event, which is why vehicles have storm shelters.

Galactic cosmic rays give a steady background dose; solar particle events can add a large dose in hours. When a solar particle storm is forecast or detected, crew should move to the shelter location and stay until the all-clear. Space-weather alerts can come from NASA's DONKI service.
