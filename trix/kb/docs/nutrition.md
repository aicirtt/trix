Fluid intake should be no less than 2,500 mL per day for men and 2,100 mL per day for women; maintaining fluid intake is important for crew health.

Calcium intake should be 1,000 to 1,200 mg per day to limit bone and calcium imbalance, and must not exceed 2,500 mg per day.

Vitamin D intake should be 25 micrograms (1,000 IU) per day because there is no sunlight to make it; if food does not supply it, a supplement is used.

Food is fixed for the whole mission, so tracking what is eaten and closing gaps early matters more than on Earth.
