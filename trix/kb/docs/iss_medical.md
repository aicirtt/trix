The ISS Crew Health Care System has three parts: health maintenance (medical care, life support and monitoring), environmental health (air, water, microbes, noise and radiation) and countermeasures (exercise and fitness checks).

Onboard devices include an ultrasound unit, a defibrillator with heart monitor, a Holter ECG, wrist actigraphy watches, an ophthalmoscope, blood and urine sample kits and a medicine supply.

The crew medical officer is often not a physician; they follow a step-by-step medical checklist and, on the ISS, consult flight surgeons on the ground in real time. At Mars distances that real-time help is not available, which is the gap TRIX fills.
