title: NASA OCHMO-TB-004 - Carbon Dioxide (NASA-STD-3001)
url: https://www.nasa.gov/ochmo-tb-004-carbon-dioxide-2/
---
Spacecraft rely on CO2 removal equipment because closed cabins cannot vent exhaled CO2 naturally.

NASA-STD-3001 Volume 2 requires the one-hour average CO2 partial pressure in the habitable volume to stay at or below 3 mmHg. Keeping the seven-day average below about 2.5 mmHg keeps the risk of headache low.

Headaches have been reported between about 2.8 and 4.5 mmHg, with fatigue, poor sleep and nausea at higher levels. Fluid shift in microgravity causes similar symptoms, so headaches should be checked against cabin CO2 before assuming illness.

Practical steps: sleep near a ventilation outlet, report high readings so the crew can check the scrubber, and log headaches so patterns can be found.
