title: NASA HRP - Behavioral health, isolation and teamwork risks
url: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/
---
NASA lists behavioral changes and psychiatric disorders, inadequate teamwork, and inadequate sleep and irregular schedules among the human health risks of spaceflight.

Living in a small space for long periods, far from family, can bring loneliness, stress, low mood and tension between crewmates. NASA's countermeasures include carefully selecting, training and supporting crews so they can work as a team for months or years.

Useful steps when feeling lonely, stressed or low: talk to a crewmate or your buddy, protect sleep and leisure time, and ask for a private call with the behavioural health team. Persistent low mood should be raised with the crew medical officer.
