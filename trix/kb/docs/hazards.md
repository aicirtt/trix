title: NASA HRP - Five hazards of human spaceflight
url: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/
---
NASA's Human Research Program groups spaceflight dangers into five hazards: space radiation, isolation and confinement, distance from Earth, gravity fields, and hostile or closed environments.

Distance from Earth makes instructions, supplies and medical care harder to get the farther a crew travels; a Mars mission could last about three years, so planning and self-sufficiency are key.

Gravity fields affect muscles, bones, the inner ear and organs, which must adapt to weightlessness, to partial gravity such as Mars (about three-eighths of Earth's) and back again on return.

The hazards can combine and make each other's effects worse.
